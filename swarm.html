Swarm Removal


Mississippi Queen Farms is a Saint Louis Based company that proudly offers honeybee swarm removal and honey bee hive cut outs to metro STL and the surrounding areas. Distances longer than 30 miles may be subject to additional travel fees, and all jobs are by quote. We would be very happy to provide this service to your business or residence. We are BLS (basic life support) and CPR certified. Additionally an epi pen for our personal use is always ready in the vehicle. We can provide a waiver with release of liability at request. All removed bees are taken to our apiary and allowed to live their life without disturbing any humans.
